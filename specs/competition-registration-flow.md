# Competition registration flow

How an athlete registers for a competition ("giải đấu"). This is the **design spec** transcribed from the process flowcharts — a frontend wizard flow plus its backend checks — reconciled against the actual schema and the existing purchase code so it can be built directly. 

Throughout, "user" = athlete. "CCCD" is the Vietnamese citizen ID card. "Giải" / "giải đấu" = the competition/meet.

## 0. Data model changes this feature introduces

These were previously unmodelled or contradicted the schema. Build them in [schema.ts](../lib/external/drizzle/migrations/schema.ts) (source of truth — SQL is generated by drizzle-kit, never hand-edited) and reuse the constants in [lib/constants/constants.ts](../lib/constants/constants.ts).

- **`user_violations`** — change from one-row-per-athlete to **many-rows-per-athlete**: drop the `vpfId` primary key, add a `serial id` primary key, keep `vpfId` as an FK, keep `note` and `expireYear`. The violation **level is the count of that athlete's rows whose `expireYear >= meet.systemYear`** (see §2).
- **`competition_ban_list`** — new table: `id serial PK`, `meetId` FK → meets, `vpfId` FK → users, `reason text`, unique `(meetId, vpfId)`. The per-competition exclusion list; `reason` is shown verbatim in the block popup (§2).
- **`meets.entryFee`** — new `integer` column (VND). The competition entry fee, set per meet.
- **`users.competitionPhotoUrl`** — new `text` column. The athlete's competition profile picture, uploaded to R2 with `uploadVipImage` and reused across meets (§4).
- **`competition_purchase_metadata.mediaPlus`** — new `boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false` column. Whether the Media Plus add-on was bought (§5).
- **`purchases.type`** — change from a single `purchase_type` enum to a **`purchase_type[]` array**, so one purchase record can span several categories (e.g. `['competition', 'vpf_membership']`) paid with one transfer. Existing VIP purchases become `['vip']`. `approvePurchase` dispatches on the array (§7). Media Plus is **not** a type — it is the boolean above, its cost folded into the combined amount.

Doping uses the existing `users.drug_violate` boolean — no new table.

## 1. Identity gate

The wizard **starts with the identity verification step** — it is a mandatory step, not a redirect and not optional. Submitting the form is required to register; **admin approval is not** (an unapproved athlete still pays and lands in "submitted / pending review", §7). The gate keys off `users.identityVerified` (CCCD admin-approved) and the 4-state `identity_verifications.status` — see [registration & identity verification](identity-verification.md).

Registration is only offered when the meet's registration window is open — today within `[meets.startRegistration, meets.closeRegistration]` — otherwise the wizard is closed with a "registration not open" notice. Email must be verified first (a precondition of submitting identity at all), otherwise the wizard sends the athlete to verify their email.

- **Already verified** (`identityVerified === true`, status `approved`) → the form renders **read-only** so the athlete reviews their saved personal info, then the ban-list checks (§2) apply.
- **Not verified yet** (`not_submitted` / `pending` / `rejected`) → the athlete **fills the verification form inline, in the wizard**, and cannot continue until it is submitted (status `pending` or `approved`). The ban-list checks are **skipped** on this path (at this point the system considers the non-verified athlete a new athlete, so they can't be banned beforehand).

The same form component backs both this step and the standalone verification page, so there is one implementation. Because the athlete's `dob` only reaches `users` on approval, the age-derived options in §4 read `users.dob ?? identity_verifications.dob` — which is why submitting the form first is what makes the division list computable.

## 2. Ban-list checks (verified athletes only)

Run in order; each is a gate. The backend looks up the athlete's verified CCCD.

1. **User violation ban** — the athlete's **level is the number of `user_violations` rows whose `expireYear` is greater than or equal to the competition's `systemYear`** (expired violations do not count). Three outcomes:
   - **Level ≥ 2** → blocked: show notice and end.
   - **Level 1** → allowed to continue, but the athlete must **write a commitment/pledge and pay a fine** ("viết cam kết, đóng phạt"); the page shows a notice about this, and an email is sent manually to handle both.
   - **Level 0 (no active violation)** → continue.
2. **Doping ban** — if `users.drug_violate === true`, show notice and end; otherwise continue.
3. **Competition ban list** ("ban list giải") — the per-competition exclusion list in `competition_ban_list`. If the athlete has a row for this meet, show the popup below and end; otherwise proceed to registration.

The block popup (literal UI copy, `A`/`B` are the athlete name / meet name; the reason after "Lý do:" is the row's `reason` text):

> **Vận động viên A** nằm trong danh sách không được đăng ký **giải B**. Lý do: {reason}. Nếu phát hiện nhầm lẫn, vui lòng liên hệ VPF tại đây (chèn link page contact).

(*Athlete A is on the list of those not allowed to register for competition B. Reason: {reason}. If you believe this is a mistake, please contact VPF here.*)

`reason` is free text authored per ban (e.g. "đã giành HCV hạng cân 74 tại giải đấu X" / "đã tham gia thi đấu tại giải đấu Y").

## 3. Registration capacity: VPF member vs Guest lifter

An option button chooses how the athlete competes:

- **VPF member** — must accept the **VPF membership terms** ("Điều khoản membership VPF…"), a mandatory checkbox, then continue.
- **Guest lifter** — for athletes who are members of **another IPF-affiliated powerlifting federation**. Skips the membership-terms step. This path is only offered when `meets.allowGuestRegistration` is true; otherwise the meet is member-only.

Both paths then hit the mandatory **data-usage consent** checkbox ("Tôi đồng ý v/v sử dụng dữ liệu…") — required to proceed to the next step.

## 4. Competition entry details ("Điền thông tin thi đấu")

The athlete fills a competition data form:
- Sporting gender (`sex`) — not stored on the athlete, chosen here; written to `competition_purchase_metadata.sex`.
- Weight class → `competition_purchase_metadata.weightClass`
- Age division → `competition_purchase_metadata.division`
- Rack pin, foot block (`users.squatRackPin` / `benchRackPin` / `benchSafetyPin` / `benchFootBlock`, optional)
- Competition profile picture (`users.competitionPhotoUrl`, uploaded to R2)

The system derives the valid options from the athlete's real data — the athlete does not pick weight class / division freely:

- **Weight class** ("Hạng cân") — system uses the chosen sporting gender + real age to show only the weight classes valid for that gender (`WEIGHT_CLASS_MALE` / `WEIGHT_CLASS_FEMALE`).
- **Age category** ("Độ tuổi") — backend computes real age from `dob` (a year) against `meets.systemYear` to show only valid divisions. Example: a 2026 competition with an athlete born in 2006 (age 20) shows only **Junior / Open**.

See [Powerlifting domain rules](../memory/powerlifting-domain-rules.md) for the weight-class and division definitions this must line up with.

## 5. Add-ons

Optional extras registered before payment: **Media Plus**. Selecting it sets `competition_purchase_metadata.mediaPlus = true`; its cost (a constant fee) is added to the combined amount in §7. No standalone purchase row.

## 6. VPF membership fee

If the athlete registers as a **VPF member** and has **not** paid the membership for the competition's year (`users.vpfMembershipExpiresAt` earlier than the meet's year), a **200,000 VND** membership fee is owed and folded into the combined payment. On approval, membership is extended (and a `vpf_membership_purchase_metadata` row written for the covered `membershipYear`). Guest lifters do not pay this.

## 7. Payment and confirmation

Payment mirrors the [VIP purchase flow](vip-purchase-flow.md) — Vietnamese bank transfer, not a card processor — but as **one combined purchase** covering every owed category:

- Backend creates **one `purchases` row** with `type` = the array of owed categories (always includes `competition`; adds `vpf_membership` when §6 applies), `amount` = entry fee (`meets.entryFee`) + membership fee (if owed) + Media Plus fee (if chosen), and a random unique **6-digit** transaction ref code (`refCode`, matching SePay's `VPF\d{6}`).
- It writes the `competition_purchase_metadata` row (meet, sex, weight class, division, `mediaPlus`) and, when membership is owed, the `vpf_membership_purchase_metadata` row.
- Frontend shows the matching **VietQR image** (memo `VPF<refCode>`) and **auto-refreshes in the background** until the app auto-detects the incoming transfer (SePay webhook), then shows the result.
- `approvePurchase` **dispatches on the type array**: for `competition` it confirms the entry; for `vpf_membership` it extends `users.vpfMembershipExpiresAt`; for `vip` it extends `users.vipMembershipExpiresAt` (existing behaviour). It stays the single activation point for the SePay webhook and the admin approve/cancel endpoints.

The final message depends on verification status:

- **CCCD already admin-verified** → success:
  > Xin cảm ơn và chúc mừng bạn đã thành công đăng ký tham gia thi đấu tại giải đấu A.

  (*Thank you and congratulations — you have successfully registered to compete at competition A.*)

- **CCCD not yet admin-verified** → pending review:
  > Xin cảm ơn bạn đã đăng ký tham gia thi đấu tại giải đấu A. Trạng thái đăng ký hiện tại là "Đã gửi thông tin đăng ký". Thông tin đăng ký của bạn sẽ được VPF kiểm tra trong thời gian sớm nhất có thể. Nếu sau 7-10 ngày làm việc kể từ thời điểm hiện tại, nếu bạn chưa nhận được email xác nhận đăng ký hoặc trạng thái đăng ký của giải đấu A tại đây (chèn link trang quản lý tài khoản web VPF) vẫn chưa được cập nhật thành "Đăng ký thành công", xin vui lòng liên hệ VPF để được kiểm tra và giải quyết.

  (*Thank you for registering for competition A. Your current status is "Registration submitted". VPF will review it as soon as possible. If after 7–10 working days you have not received a confirmation email, or the status here has not updated to "Registration successful", please contact VPF.*)

So an **unverified** athlete can still pay and register, but lands in a *submitted / pending admin review* state rather than *confirmed*.
